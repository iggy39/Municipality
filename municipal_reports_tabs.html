What this page shows

Comparison Overview

This is the best page for a quick management view. It compares all 10 municipalities and shows how strong or weak each one looks across reviewed publication categories.

  • Municipality cards show total resident-facing inventory items and the strongest service areas for each municipality.
  • The quality badges show how many categories were rated high, medium, low, blocked, or missing.
  • The category table shows which publication areas are generally strong or weak across the full group.
  • The reliability notes explain important limits, such as blocked pages and auxiliary channels.

Use this tab when you want to compare municipalities side by side and understand overall publication quality.

What this page shows

Resident Birdseye

This page translates municipal pages and files into resident service meanings. It asks what the item is about, not only what file type it is.

  • The top metrics show total classified items, service categories, unclear items, and naming aliases.
  • The municipality cards show each city's main service areas, discovery mix, and source mix.
  • The heatmap shows how many items each municipality has in each resident-facing service category.
  • The usefulness table separates detailed service content from forms, directories, document repositories, and unclear matches.
  • The examples show real titles that explain why service-based labels are easier to understand than raw document-format labels.

Use this tab when you want to understand what residents can actually find or do on municipal sites.

What this page shows

Raw Inventory

This is the most detailed evidence page. It counts the municipal web pages and downloadable documents found during the bounded crawl.

  • The totals table shows each municipality's total items, downloadable files, and web pages.
  • The heading table shows counts grouped by broad citizen-facing headings.
  • The document subtype table shows normalized file or page types, such as forms, protocols, reports, tenders, and service pages.
  • The committee breakdown shows which committee-related records were found most often.
  • The normalization section keeps raw Hebrew examples visible so the grouping remains auditable.
  • The crawl notes explain discovery limits, including sitemap coverage and crawl caps.

Use this tab when you need audit evidence, raw counts, or details behind the higher-level summaries.
